Club Roles and Responsibilities

President

Oversees all club activities and ensures smooth operation.

  • Leads club meetings
  • Represents the club at university events
  • Coordinates with faculty advisors

Vice President

Assists the president and manages internal operations.

  • Takes over when president is unavailable
  • Manages member participation
  • Organizes internal events

Operation Heads

Manages the technical aspects of drone operations.

  • Maintains drone equipment
  • Ensures safety protocols
  • Trains new members
